# Code of Conduct

We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or experience level.

## Standards

- Be respectful, constructive, and kind.
- Value differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Focus on what is best for the overall community.
- Harassment, trolling, derogatory comments, or unwelcome behavior will not be tolerated.

## Reporting & Questions

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please reach out directly to **@mythicavalon on Discord**.

All concerns will be reviewed promptly and handled with confidentiality and care.
